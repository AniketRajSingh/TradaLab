Aim: Determine the Equivalent Resistance of Two Resistors in Series and Parallel.


Theory:

In electrical circuits, resistors can be connected in series or parallel. The equivalent resistance depends on the configuration.

Series Connection: \( R_{\text{eq}} = R_1 + R_2 \)

Parallel Connection: \( \frac{1}{R_{\text{eq}}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} \)

Materials Required

  • Two resistors
  • Ammeter
