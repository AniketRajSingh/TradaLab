Resistance Calculator Using Color Codes

Aim:

To determine the resistance of a resistor based on its color code. The color bands on a resistor indicate its resistance value, tolerance, and sometimes reliability.

Theory:

Resistors are color-coded to indicate their resistance values. Each color represents a specific number, and the arrangement of the colors determines the resistor's value. The first two colors represent significant digits, the third color represents the multiplier, and the fourth color represents tolerance (optional).

Color Code Chart:

  • Black: 0
  • Brown: 1
  • Red: 2
  • Orange: 3
  • Yellow: 4
  • Green: 5
  • Blue: 6
  • Violet: 7
  • Gray: 8
  • White: 9
  • Gold: Multiplier x0.1, Tolerance ±5%
  • Silver: Multiplier x0.01, Tolerance ±10%
