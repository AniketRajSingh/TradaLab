Aim: Determine the Equivalent Voltage of Two Batteries in Series and Parallel.


Theory:

In electrical circuits, batteries can be connected in series or parallel. The equivalent voltage depends on the configuration.

Series Connection: \( V_{\text{eq}} = V_1 + V_2 \)

Parallel Connection: \( V_{\text{eq}} = V_1 \) (assuming identical batteries)

Conclusions

  • In series connection, the equivalent voltage is the sum of the individual voltages.
  • In parallel connection, the equivalent voltage is the same as the voltage of the individual batteries (assuming identical batteries).
